# 📱 Staff Guide — Daily Stock Entry

**Midori No Mart — Fresh Produce**

Thank you for your help! Please record the stock every day. It only takes a few minutes.

> ⚠️ **VERY IMPORTANT: Please enter every number carefully and double-check before you Save.**
> These numbers are used to decide reordering, sales, and profit. A wrong number causes
> wrong decisions. Take your time, count carefully, and check once more before saving.

> 🟢 **THE #1 RULE — about "Stock":**
> **"Stock" is how many are LEFT (remaining on the shelf) — NOT how many were sold.**
> Count what is physically on the shelf at the end of the day, and type THAT number.
> ✅ Correct: 12 cucumbers left on the shelf → type **12**
> ❌ Wrong: 5 cucumbers were sold today → do NOT type 5
> (You never enter the sold amount. The system calculates sales automatically.)

## The 4 boxes (what each one means)

Each product has colored boxes:

| Box | Color | What to enter |
|---|---|---|
| **Stock** | 🟢 Green | How many are LEFT right now |
| **Waste** | 🔴 Red | How many you THREW AWAY (rotten / damaged) |
| **Tasting** | 🔵 Blue | How many you used for IN-STORE TASTING |
| **Baked** | 🟠 Orange | (Sweet Potato only) How many you BAKED into yaki-imo |

> Only **Sweet Potato (Raw)** has the orange **Baked** box. Other items have 3 boxes.

---

## How to enter (every day, after closing)

1. Open the app.
2. Check the **Date** at the top (today is shown by default).
3. Type **your name**.
4. For each product, fill the boxes:
   - **Stock** = count what is LEFT on the shelf, and type that number. (Sold out → type 0)
   - **Waste** = if you threw any away, type how many. (If none, LEAVE IT BLANK)
   - **Tasting** = if you used any for sampling, type how many. (If none, LEAVE IT BLANK)
   - **Baked** = (Sweet Potato only) if you baked some, type how many. (If none, LEAVE IT BLANK)
5. Tap the green **"Save today's stock"** button.
6. You will see "Saved ... items. Thank you!" — done! ✅

---

## Important rules

- ⚠️ **Enter carefully. Double-check every number before you Save.** Accuracy is the most important thing.
- ✅ **Stock** = what is LEFT, NOT what was sold.
- ✅ Enter once a day, at the same time (after closing).
- ✅ You only need to fill **Waste / Tasting / Baked** when it actually happened. If none, **leave it blank** (no need to type 0).
- ✅ If you make a mistake, just enter the correct number again and Save (it overwrites today).
- ❌ Do NOT enter the amount sold.
- ❌ Do NOT guess the stock — please count.

---

## Examples

| Situation | What to enter |
|---|---|
| 12 cucumbers left | Cucumber → Stock 12 |
| Tomatoes sold out | Tomato → Stock 0 |
| 3 apples went rotten, 25 left | Apple → Stock 25, Waste 3 |
| Gave 5 grapes for tasting, 8 left | Grape → Stock 8, Tasting 5 |
| Baked 10 sweet potatoes, 90 raw left | Sweet Potato (Raw) → Stock 90, Baked 10 |

## About Baking (Sweet Potato) 🍠 — IMPORTANT

When you make yaki-imo, you use TWO items in the app. Do both:

**STEP 1 — On "Sweet Potato (Raw)":**
- In the orange **Baked** box, enter HOW MANY you baked today.
  (Example: you baked 10 → type 10 in Baked.)
- In the green **Stock** box, enter how many RAW are LEFT.

**STEP 2 — On "Sweet Potato (Baked)":**
- In the green **Stock** box, enter how many BAKED (yaki-imo) are LEFT on the shelf.

That's it. The system automatically moves the baked ones from raw to baked. ✅

> Simple rule:
> - "How many you BAKED" → Baked box on **Sweet Potato (Raw)**
> - "How many baked are LEFT" → Stock box on **Sweet Potato (Baked)**
